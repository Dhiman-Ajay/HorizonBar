# Privacy Policy for HorizonBar

**Effective Date:** April 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HorizonBar ("we", "us", or "our") handles your data. Our primary goal is to provide a seamless tab management experience while strictly respecting your privacy.

## 1. Data Collection
**HorizonBar does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any of your personal data.** 
All operations are performed locally on your device. We do not use analytics, tracking scripts, or telemetry to monitor your usage of the extension.

## 2. Data Storage
Any data required for the extension to function (such as your preferred bar height, pinned state, scroll position, and other layout settings) is stored **locally** on your device using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API. This data is never sent to our servers or any third parties.

## 3. Permissions Justification
To provide its core functionality, HorizonBar requires the following permissions. None of these are used to collect or share your data:

*   **Tabs & ActiveTab:** Required to read the list of your open tabs, detect tab changes (creation, updates, removal), and identify the currently active tab to display them in the HorizonBar interface.
*   **Storage:** Required to save your layout preferences and settings locally on your machine.
*   **Scripting:** Required to manage the docking behavior and positioning of the custom tab bar window.
*   **System.Display:** Required to calculate your screen dimensions to ensure the browser window is correctly positioned below the floating tab bar.
*   **Favicon:** Required to fetch and display website icons (favicons) next to your tab titles in the bar.

## 4. Third-Party Services
HorizonBar does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or trackers. Your browsing activity remains entirely private to your machine.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
If we make any significant changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.

## 6. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support tab.
